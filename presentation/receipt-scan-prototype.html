Field mapping & handoff notes — mobile draft → desktop → backend

2.Same validation & tax engine. Net/Tax and all rules come from calc.js / before('SAVE') — mobile never computes money itself.

4.Submit is desktop-only. Mobile hides Apply-for-Approval; the identical submitClaim path runs on desktop.
